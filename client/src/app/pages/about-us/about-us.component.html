Programme Development and Quality Assurance (PDQA)

The Department of PDQA is responsible for leading, coordinating and managing all programme development activities (both new and revised programmes) up to the point of registration of the resultant qualifications on the National Qualifications Framework (NQF) .

Vision

To be a port of call that promote excellence through technological, innovative and sustainable programme development practices at NUST.

Mission

To facilitate the development of programmes and qualifications applying internationally benchmarked best practices in response to national needs.

Values

  • Accountability

    We take full responsibility for all our decisions and actions.

  • Excellence

    We strive for quality and professionalism in all our actions.

  • Teamwork

    We foster a culture of teamwork in pursuit of our mission.

  • Systematic

    We strive for consistency in all our activities.

  • Integrity

    We ensure honesty and moral principles in our processes and systems.

Roles and Functions

  • 01

    Provide guidance, and offer support to academic staff in the design, development, review, implementation, and management of academic programmes (curricula) and short courses in compliance with the requirements of relevant regulatory frameworks.

  • 02

    Develop, review, and ensure the effective implementation of the NUST Curriculum Framework, and related to programmes and courses’ policies, processes, and guidelines.

  • 03

    Review and provide recommendations on programmes, short courses, and curriculum content.

  • 04

    Coordinate the registration of qualifications on the National Qualifications Framework (NQF).

  • 05

    Conduct orientations and capacity-building interventions for academic staff on curriculum development and the NQF.

  • 06

    Manage programme and qualification databases.

  • 06

    Conduct verification of programmes and qualifications data in the institutional and quality assurance agencies’ documentation.

  • 08

    Conduct research related to curriculum development to improve curriculum development best practices.

  • 09

    Advise on the Programme Qualification Mix (PQM) to align with the University's mandate.ang
